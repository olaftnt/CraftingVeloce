# Two things the counter must get right about the player's own pockets.
#
# A) FINISHED ITEMS IN THE POCKET ARE NOT CRAFTING OUTPUT.
#    Report: "with 10 doors in my inventory it shows I can craft +10 doors, even though I
#    cannot." A plan that consumes the stock it finds will satisfy a request for 10 doors
#    from those very doors without crafting anything, and the "+N" then reads as if the
#    network produced them. The rig carries 10 oak doors and cannot make doors, so any
#    non-zero answer for `oak_door` is that bug.
#
# B) RAW MATERIALS IN THE POCKET ARE A LEGITIMATE SOURCE.
#    Report: "it forgot to count items from the player's inventory - you can craft from
#    them too." The rig carries 64 diorite and NOTHING else anywhere, and diorite_slab /
#    diorite_stairs are made FROM diorite, so they must come out non-zero.

/cv guitest setup
expect: rig at
wait: 120

/cv guitest open
expect: terminal screen opened
wait: 80

# A) the doors: must NOT be counted as craftable
/cv guitest search oak_door
expect: search phrase
wait: 120
/cv guitest result
expect: withoutCounts=

# B) the diorite: must be counted, from the pocket alone
/cv guitest search diorite
expect: search phrase
wait: 120
/cv guitest result
expect: withoutCounts=
